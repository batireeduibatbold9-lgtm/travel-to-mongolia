Cultural Insights & Essential Tips

What you should know before stepping into a Mongolian Ger.

Always step over the threshold of a Ger; never step on it as it represents bad luck for the host family. Receive food or gifts using your right hand or both hands supported at the elbow.
Temperatures drop rapidly at night even in summer. Pack thermal base layers, windproof jacket, durable trail shoes, high SPF sunscreen, power banks (since ger electricity is limited), and dust masks for desert drives.
June to September offers pleasant weather. July 11–15 is the premier time for Naadam Festival, featuring wrestling, archery, and horse racing.
